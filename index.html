GUARDIAN ANGEL CAMPUS • IN DEVELOPMENT

Campus safety designed around awareness and controlled access.

Guardian Angel Campus is a child-safety and supervised-facility concept that combines assigned wearable devices with campus-aware infrastructure to support authorized safety zones, event alerts, tamper awareness, and emergency response coordination.

Assigned wearableCampus gatewaysAuthorized alertsPrivacy by design

Campus-aware architecture

The concept pairs assigned wearable devices with facility-side gateways and software intended to support awareness of approved zones and defined safety events.

Authorized response

Event information is intended for authenticated, role-based personnel rather than unrestricted public access or consumer location sharing.

Privacy principle

The design goal is to minimize unnecessary location exposure and favor event-driven access, authorization controls, auditability, and data minimization.

Development status

Concept engineering and prototype development. Final performance, materials, battery life, location accuracy, cybersecurity, child-safety, and regulatory claims require testing and validation.

SAFE ENTRY DETECTION • RESEARCH CONCEPT

Earlier awareness for protected spaces.

This research program explores non-invasive sensing approaches intended to identify ammunition-related threat indicators before entry into protected facilities.

Non-invasive sensingLayered securityPrivacy-aware screeningResearch & feasibility

Goal

Improve situational awareness at controlled entry points without relying on a single signal, single detector, or automated conclusion.

Design direction

Evaluate safe sensing, anomaly detection, environmental controls, and human review as a layered system.

Responsible deployment

Any future system would require measured false-positive and false-negative rates, privacy review, operational procedures, safety testing, and applicable regulatory compliance.

Status

Research concept only. Knight International does not represent this program as a validated, approved, or commercially available detection system.

ATMOSPHERIC WATER SYSTEMS • CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

Turn humidity into a usable resource.

Knight International is exploring modular systems that capture moisture from air, condense it, and move it through appropriate treatment stages for clean, usable water.

K.I.S. VANGUARD • KI-001 • LONG-HORIZON AEROSPACE R&D

Engineering beyond the horizon.

K.I.S. Vanguard is Knight International’s long-horizon aerospace research program exploring reusable atmospheric and suborbital engineering concepts with an emphasis on crew safety, controllability, systems integration, and testable engineering.

Reusable design studyAtmospheric flightSuborbital researchCrew systems

Airframe & flight

Explore aircraft-like and lifting-body design approaches as research directions for controllability and atmospheric return.

Crew protection

Study pressure support, thermal protection, life-support interfaces, communications, and emergency systems as integrated requirements.

Avionics & control

Research navigation, flight computers, sensing, power distribution, communications, fault handling, and redundancy concepts.

Development status

Early engineering research and concept development. KI-001 is not represented as certified, flight-tested, flight-ready, or commercially available.
