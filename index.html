GUARDIAN ANGEL • IN DEVELOPMENT

Protection designed to stay discreet.

Guardian Angel is a child-safety wearable and campus-monitoring concept built around secure emergency awareness, authorized access, and privacy-conscious design.

Wearable safetyCampus monitoringPrivacy by designTamper awareness

Wearable concept

Low-profile bracelet design with secure electronics, location/proximity capability, tamper sensing, and a protected closure concept.

Campus concept

Authorized staff interfaces and campus edge gateways are envisioned to support facility safety zones, alerts, and response workflows.

Privacy principle

The design goal is not constant public tracking. Access should be authenticated, role-controlled, event-driven, and auditable.

Development status

Concept engineering and prototype planning. Final performance, compliance, materials, and safety claims require testing and validation.

SAFE ENTRY DETECTION • RESEARCH CONCEPT

Earlier awareness for protected spaces.

This research program explores non-invasive sensing approaches intended to identify ammunition-related threat indicators before entry into places such as schools and other protected facilities.

Non-invasive sensingLayered securityPrivacy-aware screeningResearch & feasibility

Goal

Improve situational awareness at controlled entry points without relying on a single signal or a single technology.

Design direction

Evaluate combinations of safe sensing, anomaly detection, environmental controls, and human review rather than treating one detector as infallible.

Responsible deployment

Any future system would need measured false-positive/false-negative rates, privacy review, operational procedures, and applicable regulatory compliance.

Status

Research concept only. No claim is made here that a validated commercial detection system currently exists.

ATMOSPHERIC WATER SYSTEMS • CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

Turn humidity into a usable resource.

Knight International is exploring systems that capture moisture from air, condense it, and move it through appropriate treatment stages for clean, usable water.
