GUARDIAN ANGEL • IN DEVELOPMENT

Protection designed to stay discreet.

Guardian Angel is a child-safety wearable and campus-monitoring concept built around secure emergency awareness, authorized access, tamper awareness, and privacy-conscious design.

Wearable safetyCampus monitoringPrivacy by designTamper awareness

Guardian Angel Wearable

A low-profile child-friendly bracelet concept with secure electronics, location/proximity capability, tamper sensing, and a protected closure design.

Guardian Angel Campus

A school and supervised-facility concept using individually assigned wearables, campus gateways, safety zones, alerts, and authorized response workflows.

Privacy principle

The goal is not unrestricted continuous tracking. Access should be authenticated, role-controlled, event-driven, limited to authorized users, and auditable.

Development status

Concept engineering and prototype development. Final performance, materials, safety, compliance, battery life, and location claims require physical testing and validation.

SAFE ENTRY DETECTION • RESEARCH CONCEPT

Earlier awareness for protected spaces.

This research program explores non-invasive sensing approaches intended to identify ammunition-related threat indicators before entry into places such as schools and other protected facilities.

Non-invasive sensingLayered securityPrivacy-aware screeningResearch & feasibility

Goal

Improve situational awareness at controlled entry points without relying on a single signal or a single technology.

Design direction

Evaluate safe sensing, anomaly detection, environmental controls, and human review as a layered system rather than treating one detector as infallible.

Responsible deployment

Any future system would need measured false-positive and false-negative rates, privacy review, operational procedures, and applicable regulatory compliance.

Status

Research concept only. Knight International does not claim that a validated commercial detection system currently exists.

K.I.S. VANGUARD • KI-001 • LONG-HORIZON AEROSPACE R&D

Engineering beyond the horizon.

K.I.S. Vanguard is Knight International’s long-horizon aerospace research program exploring reusable atmospheric and suborbital engineering concepts with an emphasis on crew safety, controllability, and testable real-world systems.

Reusable design studyAtmospheric flightSuborbital researchCrew systems

Airframe & flight

Explore lift-body and aircraft-like control concepts intended to provide stable atmospheric handling and a controlled return profile.

Crew protection

Study pressure support, thermal protection, life-support interfaces, communications, and emergency systems as integrated design requirements.

Avionics & control

Develop the research architecture around navigation, flight computers, sensing, power distribution, communications, and system redundancy.

Development status

Early engineering research and concept development. The KI-001 is not represented as a certified or flight-ready spacecraft.
